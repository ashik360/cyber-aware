Social Engineering Awareness

Make safe decisions in a real‑world social engineering scenario

Scenario Overview

Social engineering attacks exploit human trust rather than technical vulnerabilities. In this game, you will face a realistic workplace scenario and must choose the safest actions.

Scenario

You are working at your office when you receive a phone call.

Caller: “Hello, I’m from the IT department. We detected an issue with your account. Please share your login credentials so we can fix it immediately.”

The caller sounds confident and says the issue must be resolved urgently.

What should you do?
Decision logic and scoring will be implemented using JavaScript
Feedback

Feedback will be displayed here after the user selects an option, explaining why the decision is safe or unsafe.
